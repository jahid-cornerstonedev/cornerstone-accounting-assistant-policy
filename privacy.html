This Privacy Policy is a working draft and is not final until approved by management.

1. Company identity and scope

Cornerstone Development Limited ("Cornerstone", "we", "us", or "our") owns Cornerstone Accounting Assistant (the "Application"). This policy describes the Application's handling of information during the company's internal testing phase. It applies to authorized employees who use the Application for company business.

2. Information collected and processed

The Application may process information supplied by an authorized employee or generated through the employee's work, including:

  • Bank statements and related financial files;
  • General Ledger records and accounting inputs;
  • Reconciliation results, working papers, and output files; and
  • Limited information needed to operate or configure the Application.

Employee login is not currently required. If employee profiles or authentication are introduced, the information collected and the applicable controls will be documented in an updated policy.

3. Purposes of processing

Information is processed to support internal accounting and bank reconciliation work, including importing and reviewing records, comparing transactions, producing reconciliation results, and improving internal workflows. Employees must only use the Application for authorized company purposes.

4. Current local processing and storage

Financial files and reconciliation results are currently processed and stored locally on employees' computers. Cornerstone does not currently use external OCR or AI processing services for these materials. Employees remain responsible for using company-approved computers and locations and for preventing unauthorized access to locally stored files.

5. Planned integrations and service providers

QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online integration is under development and is not represented as operational by this policy. If enabled, the Application may exchange selected accounting data with Intuit's QuickBooks Online service at an employee's direction or as configured by the company. Use would also be subject to Intuit's terms, privacy documentation, availability, and account permissions. The design for storing QuickBooks OAuth credentials has not yet been finalized.

Firebase

Firebase is planned for application settings and employee profiles, not for financial document storage. The exact services, fields, regions, retention, and safeguards have not yet been finalized.

Business Intelligence

Business Intelligence integrations may be added in the future. No such integration should be treated as operational unless separately approved and documented.

Future third-party services will be assessed before use. Canadian data residency is a company requirement; compliance of future cloud services has not yet been verified.

6. Confidentiality, access, and security responsibilities

Access is limited to authorized employees with a business need. Authorization ends when employment or permission ends. Employees must protect confidential accounting information, use reasonable care with devices and files, follow company instructions, and promptly report suspected loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure through the company's approved internal channel.

This draft does not promise particular technical controls, certifications, or security outcomes. Management will determine and approve the final security requirements.

7. Data retention and deletion

Financial data retention periods have not yet been defined. Until a retention schedule is approved, employees must follow applicable company instructions and must not delete records needed for accounting, legal, audit, or business purposes. The final policy will state retention and deletion requirements here: [RETENTION POLICY TO BE CONFIRMED].

8. Canadian data residency

Canadian data residency is a company requirement for applicable services. Cornerstone has not verified that future cloud services meet that requirement. No statement on this site should be read as a certification or confirmation of residency compliance.

9. Privacy inquiries and requests

Privacy questions, requests to review or correct information, and reports of a possible privacy incident should be directed to [OFFICIAL COMPANY CONTACT EMAIL]. The official contact details are not yet confirmed.

10. Future policy changes

Cornerstone may update this policy as the Application, integrations, data practices, and company requirements develop. Material changes will be reviewed through the company's management approval process. The effective date and final version will be added after approval.

11. Management approval

This Privacy Policy is a draft pending management approval. It does not replace company instructions, applicable law, or agreements governing a particular service.
