1. Agreement and ownership

This End-User License Agreement ("Agreement") governs authorized internal use of Cornerstone Accounting Assistant (the "Application"), which is owned by Cornerstone Development Limited ("Cornerstone"). Cornerstone retains all rights, title, and interest in the Application, its documentation, configurations, and related materials, except for rights belonging to third parties.

2. Authorized internal business use

Cornerstone grants an authorized employee a limited, revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable permission to use the Application solely for Cornerstone's internal business purposes and only while the employee has authorization. The Application is currently in internal testing, and management may limit, suspend, or change access.

Employee permissions

  • Use the Application for assigned accounting, bank statement, General Ledger, and reconciliation work.
  • Enter, review, and export company records only as needed for authorized work.
  • Follow company instructions and report suspected errors, incidents, or unauthorized access.

Restrictions

  • Do not copy, sell, rent, sublicense, publish, redistribute, or provide the Application to an unauthorized person.
  • Do not use it for personal work, outside clients, or any unlawful or unauthorized purpose.
  • Do not bypass access controls, reverse engineer, modify, or interfere with the Application except as expressly authorized by Cornerstone.
  • Do not upload or disclose company financial information to unapproved services or people.

3. Confidentiality and company data

Bank statements, General Ledger records, reconciliation results, and related accounting information are confidential company information. Employees must protect that information from unauthorized access, sharing, copying, or disclosure and must use it only for legitimate company business. Local files must be handled in accordance with company instructions and any applicable records requirements.

4. Employee responsibilities

Employees are responsible for confirming that they are authorized to use the Application, keeping their work environment secure, reviewing outputs before relying on them, and escalating issues. A permission to use the Application does not grant ownership of company data or permission to retain it after access ends.

5. Handling of exported reports

Employees are responsible for handling and storing exported financial reports according to Cornerstone Development Limited's company instructions, data handling requirements, and applicable records-management procedures. The Application is a processing and report-generation tool and does not give employees authority to retain, share, or use exported reports outside approved business purposes.

6. Accounting results and verification

The Application supports accounting workflows but does not replace professional judgment, source-document review, management approval, or required accounting controls. Employees must verify imported data, calculations, matches, exceptions, and reconciliation results before using or sharing them. Cornerstone does not make a promise under this Agreement that results will be complete, accurate, uninterrupted, or suitable for every purpose.

7. QuickBooks Online and other third-party services

QuickBooks Online integration is under development and is not currently represented as operational. If made available, use may require a valid QuickBooks Online account, Intuit authorization, and compliance with Intuit's terms and requirements. QuickBooks Online is a third-party service, and its availability, features, security, and data handling are governed by Intuit. OAuth credential storage for any future integration has not yet been finalized.

Firebase is planned for application settings and employee profiles, not financial document storage. Business Intelligence integrations may be added in the future. Employees must not treat any planned integration as available unless Cornerstone has enabled and approved it.

8. Updates and changes

Cornerstone may update, maintain, replace, suspend, or discontinue parts of the Application during internal testing. Updates may change features or workflows. Employees must use the current company-approved version and follow related instructions.

9. Termination of authorization

Authorization ends when employment ends, when permission is withdrawn, or when Cornerstone otherwise directs. On termination, the employee must stop using the Application, return or securely handle company materials as instructed, and delete or surrender local copies of company data when directed and legally appropriate. Employees must not retain credentials, exports, screenshots, or other company information for personal use.

10. No transfer or redistribution

The Application and this permission are for authorized internal use only. An employee may not transfer access or allow another person to use the Application through their account, device, files, or credentials.

11. Contact

Questions about authorization or this Agreement should be directed to info@cornerstonedevltd.com.
